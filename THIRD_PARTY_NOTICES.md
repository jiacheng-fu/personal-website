## Naresh Khatri — 3d-portfolio

<https://github.com/Naresh-Khatri/3d-portfolio>

The skills keyboard on this site is after Naresh Khatri's 3d-portfolio, an
interactive keyboard where every keycap is a skill. The idea is his, and so is
the keycap mesh: `public/models/keycap-geo.json` is the `keycap-desktop` mesh
extracted from his `skills-keyboard.spline` scene and normalised (see
`src/data/keycapGeometry.ts`). The keyboard itself was rewritten for this site
in React Three Fiber; the layout, legends, lighting and choreography are new.
His keycap click samples were used earlier and are no longer shipped.
